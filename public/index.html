About ShasheCoders Academy

Founded in 2026, ShasheCoders is a regional academic initiative hosted at Dodola Special Boarding School. We bridge the educational divide across Oromia by training motivated young minds in computational thinking, software craftsmanship, and civic problem-solving.

Mission, Vision & Strategic Goals 👈 Swipe cards 👉
🎯

Our Mission

To identify, nurture, and empower high-potential high school students across Oromia by providing world-class, zero-cost residential education in algorithmic problem solving, software engineering, and systems development.

🔭

Our Vision

To establish a premier tech incubator and research pipeline in Ethiopia, transforming the Rift Valley and Oromia into a vibrant center for software innovation, technical research, and civic problem-solving.

🚀

Strategic Goals

  • 80 Residential Seats: 100% funded housing, meals, and compute power.
  • Production Output: Ship 10+ real-world civic software applications each cycle.
  • Global Readiness: Prepare candidates for national and international tech careers.

Admission Criteria & Minimum Thresholds

  • Geographic Scope: Currently enrolled in a recognized Secondary or Special Boarding school in the Oromia Region.
  • Eligible Grades: Enrolled in Grades 9, 10, 11, or 12 with proven analytical discipline.
  • Academic Standing: Cumulative average of 85%+ or standout national examination performance.
  • Algorithmic Screening: Evaluation of linear-time search and recursive decomposition logic challenges.
