About ShasheCoders Academy

Founded in 2026, ShasheCoders is a regional academic initiative hosted at Dodola Special Boarding School. We bridge the educational divide across Oromia by training motivated young minds in computational thinking, software craftsmanship, and civic problem-solving.

Our Mission

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about the admissions process, residential accommodations, curriculum, and graduation.

🔍

Students currently enrolled in Grades 9, 10, 11, or 12 in any recognized Secondary or Special Boarding school across the Oromia Region are eligible to apply. Applicants should have a minimum cumulative average of 85% or standout national examination results.

Zero cost. The entire 7-week residential program is 100% municipal-funded through grants from the Shashemene City Administration, Dodola Town Administration, and regional partner committees. Accepted students receive complete tuition coverage, campus housing, daily meals, and compute workstation access free of charge.

No prior coding experience is required. The intake evaluation focuses on raw analytical reasoning, logic decomposition, mathematical intuition, and hunger to learn. Week 1 starts from web and internet fundamentals before accelerating into advanced data structures.

Scholars reside on-site at the Dodola Ifa Boru Special Boarding Campus in the West Arsi Zone. Housing includes supervised residential dormitories, dedicated dining facilities, uninterrupted laboratory power backup, and high-speed local networking.

Bringing a personal laptop is encouraged if you have one, but it is not required. The Dodola Special Boarding School Computer Laboratory is fully equipped with dedicated workstations, Linux environments, and developer tooling for every admitted scholar.

Scholars master Modern JavaScript / TypeScript and Python for algorithm engineering and data structures, alongside Node.js, Express, PostgreSQL, and Edge AI paradigms for production full-stack systems.

The academy operates on a disciplined Monday-to-Friday cadence. Mornings (8:30 AM – 12:00 PM) are dedicated to theoretical lectures, proof analysis, and complexity breakdown. Afternoons (1:30 PM – 5:00 PM) feature hands-on terminal drills, live code reviews, and pair debugging in the campus lab.

Applications undergo an automated and holistic merit calculation (0–100 score). Academic GPA and national exam standing contribute 60%, while the quality of your logical problem decomposition on the two algorithmic screening challenges contributes 40%. The top 80 scorers receive residential offers.

Decisions are dispatched directly through your provided Telegram Handle (@) and Email Address within 7 business days of submitting your screening evaluation. Admitted students will receive official onboarding letters and travel coordination forms.

Graduates present their capstone civic applications before municipal leadership on Demo Day and are awarded the Official ShasheCoders Academy Diploma of Honors endorsed by Dodola Special Boarding School and Addis Ababa University faculty. Top algorithmic performers receive direct university recommendations and competitive mentoring support.
